# Code of Conduct

Host-Caster is made by people who love music and radio. We want everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests,
discussions and anywhere else the project meets) to feel welcome and safe.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are new to open source, or write in a second language.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you judge.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Accept feedback the same way.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.

## What is not okay

- Harassment, insults, threats, or attacks on anyone, in public or in private messages.
- Sexual language or images, and unwanted attention.
- Discrimination of any kind, for example because of age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression,
  experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.
- Publishing other people's private information, such as an address or email, without their permission.
- Spam, trolling, and anything else a reasonable person would find out of place here.

## What happens

The project maintainers look after this code of conduct. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code,
issues and other contributions that break it, and may warn, mute or ban people, for a while or for good.

## Reporting

Tell us at [support@host-caster.com](mailto:support@host-caster.com) with "Conduct" in the subject. Every report is
read, handled in confidence, and answered. The maintainers will not hold a good-faith report against you.

## Where it applies

In every project space, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
