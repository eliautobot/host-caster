## What you may do

- Say that your software is based on Host-Caster, or works with it ("a fork of Host-Caster", "compatible with
  Host-Caster"), in plain text, as long as it is clear that it is not the official project.
- Run an unchanged copy of this repository for yourself, your family, friends or your team, and call it Host-Caster
  there.
- Use the name and logo to write about the project, in reviews, articles, talks and tutorials.

## What needs a new name

If you publish a changed version (a fork), offer it to other people as a service, or put a build in an app store, give
it **your own name, logo and app icon**, and your own app id (`com.hostcaster.app` belongs to the official apps). Change
at least:

- the name shown in the app and the web app manifest (`app/index.html`, `app/public/manifest.webmanifest`,
  `app/capacitor.config.ts`, the native projects),
- the logo and icons (`app/public/logo.svg`, `app/public/icons`, the native icons),
- the app id and, if you like, the link scheme (`APP_SCHEME`).

Please do not use a name or logo that could be mistaken for Host-Caster, and do not suggest that the project owner
supports or approves your version.
